Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026.

AetherUnzip processes the archives and files you choose on your device. The app has no account, advertising, tracking, analytics, or diagnostic upload. It does not send your archives, extracted files, passwords, or activity to AetherKiri or its service providers.

  • Files and locations. The app reads files you open and writes extracted files to the destination you choose. Saved locations and recent items can include permission bookmarks so the app can reopen them on the device. When you choose iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider handles downloading and syncing under its own privacy policy.
  • Passwords. Passwords you choose to save, their labels, and their archive associations are kept in the system Keychain. The app uses a local fingerprint to recognize the archive after a rename. Keychain syncing is disabled for these entries. Delete a saved entry in Passwords to remove it and any archive associations that use it.
  • Activity and settings. The app keeps up to 12 recent archives and 20 extraction records in local preferences. These may include file names, destinations, status, and error details. Preferences also hold settings, saved locations, and a record of an extraction in progress for recovery. In Activity → Clear Activity, you can erase recent archives and extraction history. You can also remove an individual recent archive or remove a saved folder from Locations.
  • Previews and cache. Archive previews use temporary files. Nested archives may also be staged locally while they are open. Settings → Storage → Cache Management shows preview-cache usage and lets you clear unused previews, change size limits, or control automatic cleanup. The app releases temporary files as their work ends. Automatic cleanup can remove unused previews left by interrupted sessions. Extracted files stay in the destination and can be deleted in AetherUnzip, Files, or Finder.

The app opens this policy and other external links in your browser when you choose them. The websites you visit and any information you choose to share with them are subject to their own policies.

For privacy questions, contact the project through GitHub Issues. Use that public forum for general questions and keep private files, passwords, and paths out of your post.
